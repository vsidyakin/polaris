SHARED MUSICAL DIRECTION

Every track must be an original instrumental score for the same fictional game universe.

Use a disciplined late-1980s console-action sound palette:

- Two pulse-wave-style melodic voices
- One triangle-wave-style bass voice
- One noise-based percussion voice
- Optional sparse original sampled percussion accents
- Crisp, dry, tightly controlled chiptune production
- Strong foreground melody
- Active countermelody that supports rather than duplicates the lead
- Melodic bass lines rather than sustained root notes
- Short noise-channel kicks, snares, hats and impact bursts
- Minor-key urgency balanced by heroic major-color moments
- Compact harmonic movement
- Clear rhythmic momentum
- Strong contrast between A and B sections
- Memorable motifs that can be recognized after one or two listens
- No vocals, spoken words, cinematic orchestra, realistic rock band, modern EDM drops, lush ambient pads or long reverberant tails
- No references to existing game titles, characters, composers or melodies

LOOP REQUIREMENTS

Each track must be designed as a gameplay loop:

- Begin immediately on a musically useful downbeat or pickup
- No silence at the beginning
- No long introductory sound effect
- No fade-in
- No fade-out
- No final crash, held ending chord or definitive cadence
- The final measure must lead naturally back into the first measure
- Keep percussion and ambience consistent across the loop boundary
- Aim for approximately 75 seconds of continuous musical material
- Use a clear A–A′–B–A or A–B–A′ structure
- Do not claim that a file is sample-perfectly seamless unless it has actually been inspected; record any file that will need its loop boundary trimmed later
